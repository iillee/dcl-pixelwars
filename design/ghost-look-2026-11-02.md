# Ghost look — 2026-11-02

Exploratory. No criterion. Luke asked for a taller Pac-Man ghost in red and blue, with eyes that change expression (PEAK as the reference for the eyes).

## Build

- Branch `cursor/ghost-look-0774`, on top of `cursor/bot-jumps-spike-0774`.
- Bodies: `assets/models/bots/ghost-red.glb`, `ghost-blue.glb` (team paint colors). Regenerated by `node scripts/build-ghost-glbs.mjs`. The old `ghost.glb` is unused.
- Eyes are primitives on the body (`src/client/ghostFace.ts`), not baked, so the lids and pupils can move.
- Expressions from the wire state: calm while walking, squint on the lunge wind-up, open while gliding, wide pupils when knocked, nearly shut on the rescue cloud. Occasional blink.
- Hem hovers 0.5 m off the floor. The cloud ride adds another 0.55 m so the crown reaches the hook. The old sheet sat about 1.8 m up.

## Smoke

Mesh builds, normals point outward, `tsc --noEmit` passes. A front contact sheet is at `ghost-look/expressions.png` in the run artifacts (calm, wind-up, glide, hit, rescue). No Creator Hub preview on this machine.

## Unknown

Whether the lids read as eyes or as a visor, whether the hem reads, and whether the hover is the right height. Those are for a preview pass.
